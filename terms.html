Legal & Governance

Terms of Service & Commission

Clear standards governing our custom ébénisterie agreements, warranty conditions, and white-glove fulfillment.

Section 1

Nature of Bespoke Handcraft & Timber Individuality

Every Verdant Atelier creation is fashioned by hand from organic, kiln-dried timber. Because no two trees possess identical annular rings, medullary rays, or grain contours, subtle variations in grain movement and natural mineral veining are inherent to honest ébénisterie. Such characteristics constitute the authenticity and singular fingerprint of each heirloom work.

Section 2

Commission Schedule & Deposit

Upon final confirmation of the technical design dossier and millimeter dimensions, a non-refundable deposit of 50% is required to reserve the timber billets and assign workshop bench time. The balancing 50% plus applicable delivery fees are settled upon completion of the master joiner's inspection and prior to crating and dispatch.

Section 3

The 25-Year Structural Guarantee

We guarantee the structural joinery (mortise-and-tenon sockets, dovetails, butterfly splines, and structural chassis) against delamination, joint looseness, or failure for twenty-five (25) years from delivery. This guarantee does not extend to normal surface wear and tear, intentional negligence, severe environmental abuse (such as placement immediately abutting radiant fires without climate moderation), or third-party unauthorized repairs.

Section 4

Delivery Access & Spatial Verification

The client or client's interior architect is responsible for verifying that exterior building entrances, elevators, staircase turns, and interior hallways accommodate the transit dimensions specified on the approved workshop drawings. Our concierge team can arrange pre-delivery architectural access surveys upon request.
