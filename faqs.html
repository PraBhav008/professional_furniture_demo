Knowledge & Care

Comprehensive insights regarding our artisanal joinery, biological timber oils, white-glove transit, and custom spatial commissions.

Every bespoke commission commences with an initial dialogue with our senior drafting team. We review your architectural floorplans, spatial light orientation, and material palette. Within 5 business days, we provide photorealistic 3D renders, millimeter workshop drawings, and physical timber/fabric sample chests. Upon your approval of the technical dossier and 50% deposit, our master joiners hand-select the matching timber billets and begin fabrication in our atelier.
Numbered edition pieces from our catalog (such as the Kyoto Chair or Solis Armchair) generally require 6 to 8 weeks of atelier crafting. Large-scale monolithic tables, credenzas, and custom-proportioned sectionals typically require 8 to 12 weeks. We never expedite production at the sacrifice of timber moisture stabilization or hand-rubbed oil curing.
Our Natural French Oak preserves the raw golden-blonde honey tones of slow-grown European deciduous trees, finished with a matte, non-yellowing botanical wax. Smoked Oak undergoes traditional ammonia fuming in a sealed chamber. The natural tannins inherent to the oak react chemically with the vapor, darkening the timber completely through its entire cross-section to an ebony-espresso hue without the artificial, surface-only appearance of chemical wood stains.
For daily maintenance, wipe surfaces with a clean, dry lint-free linen cloth, or a cloth barely moistened with lukewarm water. Never employ ammonia-based sprays, bleach, or silicone-based furniture polishes. Every commission is delivered with a complimentary Verdant Atelier Care Tin containing our cold-pressed organic linseed wax. Applying a light circular buff once every 12 to 18 months will replenish the protective oil and deepen the wood's natural patina.
Yes. We frequently accommodate Customer’s Own Material (COM) and Customer’s Own Leather (COL). Our technical team will inspect fabric rub-test ratings and provide exact yardage calculations with pattern-matching requirements. We also offer our signature collection of Tuscan saddle leathers and custom-loomed Belgian mohairs.
Yes. Traditional cabinetmakers designed joinery specifically to allow wood movement. We mill slotted sliding tenons and relief grooves into the undersides of our solid slabs, combined with concealed steel transverse battens with slotted holes. This allows the natural timber to expand and contract across seasonal humidity shifts (35% to 65% RH) without checking, bowing, or warping.
All domestic and international shipments travel in custom-built, shock-absorbing plywood transit crates. Our white-glove logistics partners arrange a confirmed 2-hour arrival window, carry the furniture inside to your designated room of choice (including flight-stair carries), complete all mechanical assembly, inspect leveling, and remove all packing crates for recycling.
100% of our French White Oak, European Ash, and Beech originates from PEFC™ and FSC®-certified family-owned forests in Western Europe. Trees are harvested under selective felling programs that safeguard forest canopy age and biodiversity. Each piece comes with a provenance certificate detailing the specific forest parcel number where the timber grew.
Our guarantee warrants against any structural joinery failure, tenon delamination, or internal frame distortion under normal residential use for twenty-five years from the date of hallmark stamping. In the rare event of a joinery defect, our restoration team will either repair the piece in situ or transport it back to our workshop at our expense.
Our Trade & Specification program offers tiered professional trade pricing, priority fabrication slots in our workshop calendar, direct telephone access to our senior engineering draftsmen, comprehensive 3D CAD/Revit BIM library files, and complimentary presentation swatch boxes for client meetings.
